Code of Conduct

1. Treat all colleagues with respect and professionalism.
2. Working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday.
3. Work from home is allowed up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
4. Any grievance must be reported to HR within 7 days of the incident.
5. Violation of this code may lead to disciplinary action per the PIP process.
