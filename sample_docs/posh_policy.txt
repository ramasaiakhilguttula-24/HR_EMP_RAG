POSH Policy - Prevention of Sexual Harassment

As per Article 12.3 of the company POSH policy, every office must have an
Internal Committee (IC) to handle complaints.
Complaints must be filed in writing to the IC within 3 months of the incident.
The IC must complete its inquiry within 90 days and submit a report to HR.
Non-compliance with POSH guidelines is treated as misconduct under Article 12.3.
