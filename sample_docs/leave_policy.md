# Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Sick leave is 12 days per year with a medical certificate for absences over 2 days.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per company policy for India employees.
Paternity leave is 15 days within 6 months of childbirth.

## Notice Period

Employees with less than 2 years of tenure serve 30 days notice.
Employees with 2 or more years of tenure serve 60 days notice.
